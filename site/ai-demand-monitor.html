Hyperscaler capex, contracted backlog, AI-lab commitments

Who is paying for the megawatts? The capacity tracker counts sellable AI megawatts on the supply side. This page watches the demand that fills them: what the five big buyers spend and guide, what the sellers have under contract, and what the frontier labs have committed to. Capex rows as of —, contracted rows as of —, lab register through —. Every figure carries its disclosure date and source; secondary marks a size supported only by press reports. Research, not P&L.

1 · Hyperscaler capex — reported vs guided

Cash paid for property and equipment from each 10-Q/10-K (FMP cash-flow statement), one row per reported quarter since Q1 2025 plus the Q4 2024 base. Each company's headline measure differs (Microsoft adds finance leases, Meta adds finance-lease principal, Amazon nets incentives, Oracle nets prepayments) — the basis toggle shows those alongside. q/q and trailing-4Q are computed from the company's own series. CoreWeave and Nebius are shown as buyer context and excluded from cohort totals.

Stacked bars: quarterly cash capex, $B, by calendar quarter of the period end (Oracle's Feb/May/Aug/Nov quarters are placed in the calendar quarter they end in). Bar height is the five-company total; a faded bar is a quarter not all five have reported yet, so its height is not a cohort total. Hover a segment for the quarter's guide text.

Spend so far against the full-year guide

Each company's latest stated full-year guide, against what it has spent so far in that year on the measure it guides on (its headline measure, so Microsoft and Meta include leases and the bars will not match the cash bars above). The tick is where an even quarterly pace would be. The second line is what the rest of the year averages if the company lands on its midpoint — arithmetic on the company's own guide, not a forecast. One $ scale; rows are never added up.

2 · Contracted demand — RPO, backlog and the lab register

Each seller's own metric, named verbatim: Microsoft commercial RPO and Oracle RPO (from the calls above), CoreWeave revenue backlog, Nebius RPO, IREN accounting RPO (then RPO + lease value), Applied Digital / Core Scientific / Galaxy / TeraWulf lifetime contracted lease revenue. These are not one comparable series — perimeters change (IREN 2026Q2, TeraWulf each quarter) and lifetime contract revenue is not a remaining balance. carry = an earlier disclosure carried forward, no new measurement.

AI-lab compute commitments and funding — dated register

Announcements by OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and Meta (as a buyer of external compute): counterparty, type, size as disclosed, term and source. Programs, LOIs, guarantees, funding rounds and cumulative updates overlap — the sizes are deliberately not summed, and the line under each $ figure says what it measures. Primary means the size comes from a company, lab or SEC source; secondary rows link the press report. Filter by lab or sourcing.

3 · Cross-check — buyer spend vs contracted neocloud megawatts

The denominator uses the selected operators' tenant rows from the published capacity dataset, excluding explicitly inferred MW and rows without MW. CoreWeave's tenant overlap, the ORCL/SPCX benchmarks, BTBT's parent row and the AKAM context row are excluded. The constituent snapshot and exclusions are retained in the demand dataset. The numerator is the five hyperscalers' latest reported quarter annualised. This is a comparison of disclosures for scale; it is not a capacity price, a share-of-spend measure or a forecast.
