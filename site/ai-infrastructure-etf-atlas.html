01 / Core vs satellite

Broad sleeves are not neutral.

AIPO spans power, construction, cooling and silicon; GRID leans industrial; DTCR leans real estate and towers. Each can anchor a basket, but each imports a different factor exposure.

02 / Chokepoint beta

Purity raises torque—and fragility.

DRAM, LYTE and NCLD target narrower scarcity claims. That can make the thesis legible, while increasing cycle, geography, financing and single-industry risk.

03 / Track-record trap

Do not compare unequal histories.

LYTE and NCLD have only closes in this dataset; DRAM began in April. The chart therefore opens on the window all six share; in the full-2026 view their lines are launch-to-date snapshots, not full-year evidence.

07 / Receipts

Method & sources.

Market data and fund descriptions are deliberately separated: Nasdaq supplies the closing-price series; issuers supply objectives, fees, inception dates and holdings.

Performance methodology

  • Daily unadjusted closing market prices, through the latest date shared by all six funds.
  • The default chart view rebases all six to 0% at the first close they share. The full-2026 view rebases each series at its first available 2026 close, so 2026 launches show since-inception price change.
  • Distributions, taxes, bid/ask spreads and trading costs are excluded. Issuer-reported total returns will differ.
  • Holdings change. Every exposure list carries its own as-of date and should be refreshed before making a trade.
  • Cash, currencies and money-market funds are excluded. Roundhill rows sharing the same security identifier are consolidated; the displayed weights are summed.
