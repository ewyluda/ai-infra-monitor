Method. Free cash flow is operating cash flow less purchases of property, plant and equipment, the capex line the demand monitor charts; finance leases are not in it. Each fiscal quarter counts in the calendar quarter where most of it falls (period end minus 45 days), so Nvidia's August–October quarter is Q3. TSMC and ASML convert to dollars at the Federal Reserve's average rate for the quarter. A group's total counts a quarter only when every member has reported it; a faded bar is a quarter still reporting. Nvidia is in both groups. Semis are the AI Trade Layer Index's Accelerators & Chip Design and Semi Equipment & Packaging layers, plus Micron.
