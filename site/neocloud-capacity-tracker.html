How the megawatts were normalized

Critical IT MW, not gross

The sellable unit is critical IT MW (power to energize GPUs at redundancy), ~1.2–1.5× below gross/utility nameplate. Pipeline figures quoted in GW are gross and shown only in detail.

AI MW, not bitcoin MW

Mining load is interruptible & air-cooled; converting to always-on liquid-cooled AI shrinks usable MW ~45–55%. Bitcoin self-mining MW is excluded everywhere.

EV & tenant vs landlord

EV = market cap + net debt (these names are debt-heavy). CoreWeave leases its shells from CORZ/GLXY/APLD; BTBT is WhiteFiber. The de-duplicated view drops the tenant, benchmark, parent and context rows.

Verify-adjusted, skeptical

A refute pass downgraded rosy "operational" claims to 0 where load isn't energized + billing. EV/MW & coverage are approximate (net debt at latest filing) — a relative ranking, not a price target.
