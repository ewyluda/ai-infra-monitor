Neocloud Capacity Tracker

How it's built

Sellable AI critical-IT megawatts across the pure-play GPU clouds and the ex-bitcoin-miners pivoting into AI colocation: what each is worth per megawatt, who its customers are, and when the capacity arrives. MW are verify-adjusted critical IT, hand-curated from 10-Ks, 10-Qs, 8-Ks and investor decks; every company record lists its sources. Market cap ≠ megawatts — the gap is the whole point.

Weighted MW = operational + half of construction + a quarter of planned. Energized = operational ÷ total. Backlog ÷ EV = contracted backlog over enterprise value. Totals are de-duplicated: are shown on their own rows but left out of the cohort totals and the timeline. Oracle and SpaceX are benchmarks for scale, not peers. Net debt includes finance and operating leases where disclosed; each record states its basis.
